Fun Facts and

Summary

What planet would you like to explore?

JUPITER

About 11× wider than Earth; about 1,300 Earths could fit inside it.

SATURN

Its rings are mostly ice, with some rock and dust; they span about 270,000 km—roughly 21 Earth diameters.

EARTH

The only world currently known to support life.

VENUS

Mercury is closest to the Sun, but Venus is the hottest planet. Venus has a thick carbon-dioxide atmosphere that traps heat through a powerful greenhouse effect, while Mercury has almost no atmosphere to hold heat.
